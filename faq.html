Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about tasks, submissions, rewards and referrals.

Check the current app listing and in-app terms for any charges or eligibility requirements. Do not pay anyone outside the official app for access.
Complete an available task according to its instructions and submit the required proof. The reward is subject to review and approval.
Submissions may need to be checked against the task requirements. Wait for the status to update in your account.
Review the task instructions and rejection reason shown in the app. If you believe there has been a mistake, contact support with the task details.
Use the wallet withdrawal flow in the app. Minimums, maximums, supported methods and processing conditions are shown in-app and may change.
The programme currently displays a ₹5 direct bonus, a ₹5 friend reward after their first withdrawal, and 1% lifetime task commission, subject to the current programme rules.
Use the Support page to contact the Task Gullak team. Include relevant task or transaction details, but never share your password or OTP.
