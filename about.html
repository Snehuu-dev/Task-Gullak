About us

Small tasks. Clear progress.

Task Gullak brings task opportunities, submission tracking, referrals and wallet information together in one place.

What is Task Gullak?

Task Gullak is a task-and-rewards platform. Users can explore available online tasks, follow the listed instructions, submit proof where required and track the review status of their submissions.

Our approach

We aim to make the task journey easier to understand: show the task details, explain what proof is needed and provide a place to track submissions and eligible rewards.

Use the platform responsibly

Task availability and reward eligibility can vary. Always read the instructions and current terms inside the app before participating.
