How Task Gullak works

From finding a task to tracking its reward, here's the basic journey.

01

Download or open

Open Task Gullak and sign in to your account.

02

Choose a task

Read the task requirements, reward and time limit before starting.

03

Complete & submit

Do the task as instructed and upload genuine, unedited proof when required.

04

Wait for review

Your submission is reviewed. Approved rewards are reflected according to the app rules.

Keep these things in mind

  • Read all task instructions before starting.
  • Submit only genuine proof that matches the task requirements.
  • Do not assume a submitted task is approved until its status changes.
  • Withdrawal minimums, limits and processing depend on the current in-app rules.
